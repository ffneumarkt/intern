# UI audit after the iOS restyle (2026-11-25)

Click-through of every route in Chromium (Brave) at 402×874 (iPhone 17 size, not real WebKit/iOS) and 1920×1200, logged in
as the seeded dev user (`hansi`, so the admin pages showed no data and some pages showed "Keine Berechtigung."). Screenshots
were taken, not kept. Nothing below is fixed yet. Re-test on a real iPhone: several things (Safari bars, safe areas, keyboard,
`backdrop-filter`, `100dvh`) cannot be judged in Chromium.

## Both sizes

1. **Zeitplan filter chips overflow.** On the phone the chip row ("Alle, Dichtprüfung, dynamische, Lebensdauer …") is cut off at
   the right edge with no scroll cue. Let `.ui-chips` scroll horizontally (hidden scrollbar, a fade at the edge) or wrap.
2. **Bare "Keine Berechtigung." page** (e.g. `/material/verwalten/geraete/neu` without the right): plain text on the grey page.
   Give it a centred empty-state (symbol, text, a "Zurück" action) like `ui-empty`.
3. **12-hour time in the Ruhezeit fields** ("10:00 PM") when the browser language is English. The app is German/Austrian: set
   `lang="de-AT"` on the inputs, or use a small custom 24 h picker.
4. **Required-field placeholder "Erforderlich"** is the same grey as a value; in the form rows it reads like a filled value.
   Use a lighter placeholder grey (`#c4c4c7`) and keep real values `text-foreground`.
5. **Row separators run to the card's right edge** while the card is rounded; fine on iOS, but the last separator before an
   inset button row ("60 s starten" in the check form) looks cut. Check `List`/`Row` hairlines around buttons inside lists.
6. **Buttons inside list rows** ("60 s starten", "Prüfen") are edge-to-edge or squeezed compared with the 16 px row padding.
   Give buttons in a `Row` the row's horizontal padding.
7. **Disabled "PDF öffnen"** is now solid grey but gives no hint why it is disabled besides the red message above; consider
   linking the message to the button (`aria-describedby`) and showing the reason next to it.

## Phone (402 wide)

8. **Large title wraps** for long titles ("Verwendung erfassen" at 402 px fits; "Beförderungspapier" nearly fills the
   line). Allow `text-wrap: balance` and a smaller size under 360 px.
9. **Floating tab bar and content:** the last row of long pages (Einstellungen, Impressum) scrolls under the bar. The bottom
   padding is there, but check the end of every long page on a device, and that the sticky action bar never hides inputs
   while the keyboard is open (iOS shrinks the visual viewport; the tab bar should hide while a field is focused).
10. **Scan symbol in the header** exists only on the due list. The use form, item page and check page have no quick link to
    the scanner; decide whether the symbol belongs on every Material page.
11. **Back button** says "Zurück" everywhere; iOS shows the previous page's title ("‹ Fällig"). Pass the previous title to
    `Page`'s `back` (the route table knows the titles).
12. **Swipe back and slide transitions** are untested on touch hardware (written from the spec): verify the edge swipe does
    not fight Safari's own back gesture in the browser (it is meant for the installed PWA), and that scroll positions restore.
13. **Pull to refresh** is missing on the data lists (due list, category, item history); `useResource` has `reload()`.

## Desktop (1920×1200)

14. **Navigation bar spans the full window** while the content column is 528 px: the back button sits at the far left and the
    scan symbol at the far top right, far from the content. Constrain the header content to the same column, or switch to a
    sidebar/top navigation above ~900 px.
15. **Floating tab bar at the bottom of a wide window** is far from the content and the pointer. On wide screens use a top
    tab bar or a left sidebar; keep the floating capsule for phones.
16. **Content column is narrow** (528 px) on a 1920 px screen: use ~720 px for list pages and two columns for the item page
    (facts and history), the due list and the timeline.
17. **Classic scrollbar** is visible next to the page on desktop and clips the floating bar's centring slightly; style it thin
    (`scrollbar-width: thin`) or overlay.
18. **Hover and focus states:** controls have press feedback but no hover state (rows, chips, buttons) and the focus ring is
    the browser default on some elements; add `hover:` styles at `@media (hover: hover)` and a consistent `:focus-visible` ring.
19. **Beförderungspapier on desktop:** the form is one narrow card; the stepper buttons and number field are fine, but the
    "Hin und zurück" pill and the route could use two columns.

## Second pass: admin and manage pages as `verwaltung` (phone 402 px)

Mitglieder, Kategorien, Beförderungspapier (Verwaltung), Verwalten (Geräte, Prüfarten), Neues Gerät and Neue Prüfart now show
data. Findings:

20. **Empty optional fields have no cue.** In the form rows (Neues Gerät: Seriennummer, Code; Neue Prüfart: Intervall in Monaten)
    an empty, non-required field is just a label with nothing on the right, so it does not look editable. Give every inline
    text/number field a light placeholder ("Optional" or "Eingeben"), or a pale value pill.
21. **Date fields show `mm/dd/yyyy`** (browser language), e.g. Hergestellt am, Zuletzt geprüft am. Austria expects `TT.MM.JJJJ`;
    set the input language or format the value.
22. **The sticky action bar cuts the last fields hard** ("Zuletzt geprüft …" disappears under "Gerät anlegen" with a hairline).
    Strengthen the fade above the bar (taller, opaque from the bar's top edge) so no half-visible label shows.
23. **Long labels wrap into two lines** in form rows ("Name der Station", "Bezeichnung der Flasche", "Zuletzt geprüft am
    (dynamische)", "Intervall in Monaten") while the value stays one line; align the value to the first line or give the label
    column more width (45 %) when a label is long.
24. **`/material/verwalten/geraete/neu` without `?kategorie=`** says "Keine Berechtigung." although the user has the right;
    it should say a category is missing or preselect the first manageable one.

## Checked and fine

Large titles and the collapsing title bar, filled/outline tab symbols, the ADR label, stepper buttons (same height as the
field), card widths on the due list, the boxed "Code manuell eingeben" field, solid disabled buttons, alert dialogs.
